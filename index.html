About Nazra

Bridging Imports & Canadian Markets

Our Mission

At Nazra, we specialize in bringing essential, high-quality goods directly to Canada. Our goal is simple: to connect Canadian businesses, retailers, and communities with top-tier dry foods and everyday apparel sourced through reliable global manufacturing networks.

We serve as a streamlined distribution bridge, ensuring that every product entering our supply chain meets high standards of quality, freshness, and craftsmanship.

What We Do

  • Food Distribution: Importing dry foods, tea-time bakery favorites, spices, and premium rice for Canadian retail shelves.
  • Apparel Supply: Sourcing durable, high-quality everyday garments and textiles tailored for commercial distribution.
  • Reliable Logistics: Managing the import and distribution flow to make wholesale sourcing effortless for our Canadian partners.
